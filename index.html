Jinhui Yi

I am a PhD candidate in the Computer Vision Group at the University of Bonn, supervised by Prof. Dr. Juergen Gall. My research explores multimodal video understanding, long-term action anticipation, unsupervised domain adaptation, and applications of computer vision to plant phenotyping. Previously, I completed my master's degree at the University of Hanover, supervised by Prof. Dr. Bodo Rosenhahn.
